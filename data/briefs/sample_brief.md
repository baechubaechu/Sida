# Sample Project Brief

## Project Type
Small public library / community space

## Site
A narrow urban corner lot between a busy road and a quiet residential street.

## Core Problem
The site sits between two different speeds of city life: fast traffic and slow neighborhood routines.

## Site Issues
- Noise from the main road
- Limited site width
- Need for public access
- Relationship to residential scale
- Lack of shaded outdoor waiting space

## Design Intention
Create a small public library that acts as a threshold between the busy road and the quiet neighborhood.

## Current Design Direction
The project uses a thickened wall facing the road, a quieter reading zone toward the residential side, and a small covered public pocket at the corner.
